# BitGo Europe Asset Assessment Engagement

## Overview

This folder documents confidential asset assessment work performed for **BitGo Europe**.

Due to confidentiality obligations, the full deliverables are not published. This folder is intended to contain a summary of engagement that confirms the existence and nature of the work without disclosing sensitive assessment details.

## About BitGo Europe

**BitGo** is a digital asset infrastructure company focused on custody, wallets, staking, trading, financing, and settlement services for institutional clients. BitGo also offers stablecoin infrastructure and highlights regulated custody and compliance capabilities as part of its platform.

**BitGo Europe GmbH** publicly states that it holds MiCA-relevant permissions in the EU, including crypto-asset transfer services and crypto custody-related services.

## Why This Engagement Matters

BitGo is one of the most established names in institutional digital asset infrastructure. Work performed for BitGo Europe is therefore a strong public reference for security and risk work connected to regulated digital asset operations in Europe.

This is especially relevant for:
- digital asset risk and asset assessment work
- stablecoin-related review processes
- institutional custody and infrastructure environments
- compliance-sensitive crypto operations

## Engagement Scope

**Since 2022 Softstack has conducted MiCA-compliant asset assessments for BitGo Europe covering multiple stablecoins and multiple blockchain networks.**

**This work has included assessments related to USDT, USDC, PYUSD, EUROC, EURCV, AUDD, VCHF, ZUSD, and VEUR across networks including Ethereum, Solana, Stellar, XRPL, and Polygon.**

**The engagement has also included security review, MiCA-related review, and ESG-related assessment support for stablecoin issuers.**


## Summary

This folder highlights Softstack’s confidential work for **BitGo Europe**.

It is a strong reference for Softstack’s involvement in **institutional digital asset assessment**, **stablecoin-related risk review**, and **compliance-sensitive blockchain infrastructure work**.
